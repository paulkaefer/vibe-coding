# Class Ideas: Standalone HTML Apps

Each idea is a single self-contained `.html` file (no dependencies, no build step) that a student could vibe-code in one sitting.

## Simple Games

1. **Stack Attack (a Tetris twist):** Pieces fall as usual, but each piece is a *word fragment* and completed rows only clear if they spell a real word. Easy mode uses 3-letter rows.
2. **Connect 4 vs. the Gravity Gremlin:** Classic Connect 4, but every 5th turn gravity flips and all discs "fall" up. Includes a simple AI opponent.
3. **Uno-ish (call it "Unoriginal"):** Color/number matching, skip, reverse, and draw-two against 3 computer players. A "forgot to say Uno" penalty button adds drama.
4. **Minesweeper: Grocery Edition:** Same logic, but the mines are expired items and the numbers count nearby expired items.
5. **2048 with Sticky Tiles:** Some tiles are "stuck" and can't move until merged next to. Forces actual strategy.
6. **Memory Match: Class Roster Edition:** Match names to emoji or fun facts. Students can paste their own list of pairs.
7. **Snake, but it's a Commute:** Your snake is a bus. Pick up passengers (growing), avoid potholes.
8. **Higher or Lower: Weird Stats:** Compare two odd facts ("which is taller: Eiffel Tower or 4 giraffes stacked?"). Streak counter and high score.
9. **Whack-a-Deadline:** Whack assignments as they pop up before they hit their due date. Missed ones turn red and cost lives.
10. **Tic-Tac-Toe on a 5x5 Wrap-Around Board:** Edges connect, so wins can wrap. Surprisingly tricky.

## Silly

11. **Excuse Generator:** Mad-libs style excuses for late homework, tagged by believability (1 to 5 stars). Share button copies the excuse.
12. **Pet Rock Simulator:** Feed, pet, and ignore your rock. It does nothing, but its mood text is very dramatic.
13. **Passive-Aggressive Sticky Note Maker:** Pick a situation (dishes, parking, thermostat) and a politeness-to-rage slider; it drafts the note on a yellow sticky.
14. **Dramatic Weather Reporter:** Type any weather and get a breathless news anchor script.
15. **Rate My Sandwich:** Stack ingredients on a canvas plate and receive a pompous food critic's review.
16. **Fake Loading Screen Studio:** Build absurd progress bars with funny status messages ("Reticulating splines", "Convincing the printer").
17. **Dice of Destiny:** Roll for silly life decisions ("Today you must speak only in questions").
18. **Name Your Band:** Combines random adjectives, objects, and album titles into a band name and a fake poster.

## Productivity

19. **Two-Minute Rule Tracker:** Dump tasks, flag the ones under 2 minutes, and do those first with a countdown.
20. **Meeting Cost Calculator:** Enter attendees and rough hourly rates; a live ticker shows the money burning while the meeting runs.
21. **Eisenhower Inbox Sorter:** Paste a list of tasks and drag each into urgent/important quadrants, then export as a checklist.
22. **Brain Dump Timer:** 5-minute free-write with a gentle fade-out of the text at the end so you start fresh. Optionally save before it fades.
23. **Weekly Review Wizard:** A guided 5-step form (wins, blockers, next priorities) that outputs a tidy summary to copy into email.
24. **Focus Playlist Randomizer:** Enter your own list of links or song names and it picks one so you stop browsing and start working.
25. **Pack List Builder:** Pick trip type and length; it generates a checklist you can tick off and reuse.
26. **Water and Stretch Nudges:** Configurable reminders with tiny animated exercises. No notifications needed; it just flashes the tab title.

## Class-Related

27. **Random Cold Call Picker (fair edition):** Paste a roster and it picks students without repeats until everyone has gone. Includes a "pass" option.
28. **Group Maker:** Paste names, choose group size, and get balanced random groups. Keep-apart and keep-together constraints are a stretch goal.
29. **Participation Tally:** Tap a name to log a contribution; end-of-week summary shows who has not had a chance to speak yet.
30. **Exit Ticket Collector:** Display a prompt and let students type a one-liner; responses show as a shuffled word-cloud-ish wall. (Local only, so great for a demo.)
31. **Grade What-If Calculator:** Enter weights and scores so far; see what score on the final is needed for each letter grade.
32. **Quiz Show Buzzer:** Space bar and keys for teams, lockout after the first buzz, and a reset. Scoreboard included.
33. **Study Session Planner:** Enter exam date and topics; it spreads review sessions across days with a spaced-repetition feel.
34. **Concept Match-Up:** Teacher pastes term/definition pairs; students drag to match them. Auto-generated from the same data as the memory game (#6).
35. **Citation Formatter Cheat Sheet:** Fill in fields (author, title, year, URL) and get APA/MLA output side by side.
36. **Lecture Pace Meter:** A timer that splits class time into segments (lecture, activity, discussion) and shows a progress bar for each, so you can see if you are running behind.

## Stretch / Mashups

37. **Productivity Roguelike:** Each task is an enemy; finishing it deals damage. Level up for streaks.
38. **Silly Study Buddy:** A flashcard quizzer where a cartoon character reacts to right and wrong answers.
39. **Procrastination Bingo:** Squares like "reorganized desk" and "watched one more video"; students tick off what they did and get a bingo.
40. **Pixel Pet Planner:** Complete tasks to feed a pixel creature that evolves over the week.
